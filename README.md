# Braindigger

Building AI tools for Uzbek speakers, from Tashkent.

- [GPTBot.uz](https://gptbot.uz/uz/gpt-uzbek-tilida/) - Free browser AI chat in Uzbek and Russian with no sign-up ([Russian version](https://gptbot.uz/ru/gpt-chat/)). Text only, with a daily free limit and an optional paid package; answers come from third-party models through an API. An independent project: not ChatGPT and not affiliated with OpenAI.
- [Awesome Uzbek AI](https://github.com/braindiggeruz/awesome-uzbek-ai) - A curated, link-checked list of AI resources for the Uzbek language: apps that work in Uzbek, open models and datasets, NLP tools, benchmarks, courses and communities. Suggestions and corrections are welcome through issues and pull requests.

**O‘zbekcha:** Toshkentdan turib o‘zbek tilida so‘zlashuvchilar uchun sun’iy intellekt vositalarini yaratamiz. Awesome Uzbek AI — o‘zbek tili uchun AI resurslarining tekshirilgan ro‘yxati; taklif va tuzatishlar uchun issue oching.
